Upload a face photo. See what SAIRA would recommend.

SAIRA reads your photo, scores four skin concerns, and turns them into the ingredients you need, finds matching products, removes unsafe ones, and builds a morning and evening routine.

This demo shows the recommendation layer only. Photo analysis needs the SAIRA server running (see the project README). Without it, set scores by hand under the photo box. The products are made-up samples, not real brands.
